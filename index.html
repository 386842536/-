BEAD STUDIO应用隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年3月29日

引言

欢迎使用BEAD STUDIO应用（以下简称"本应用"）。我们深知隐私对您的重要性，因此我们致力于保护您的个人信息安全。本隐私政策详细说明了本应用如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。

使用本应用即表示您同意本隐私政策的条款。如果您不同意本政策的任何部分，请不要使用本应用。

一、我们收集的信息

1.1 本地存储数据

本应用是一款单机应用，所有数据处理都在您的设备本地完成，不会上传到任何服务器。我们可能会在您的设备上存储以下信息：

  • 应用设置：包括网格大小、颜色数量、渲染模式、显示模式等用户偏好设置
  • 颜色覆盖设置：您自定义的颜色覆盖数据
  • 处理过程中的临时数据：图片处理过程中生成的临时文件和数据

1.2 权限使用

为了提供完整的功能，本应用需要访问以下系统权限：

  • 相册访问权限：用于从相册上传图片以及将导出的作品保存到相册
  • 文件系统访问权限：用于保存和管理导出的作品文件

1.3 我们不收集的信息

  • 个人身份信息：我们不会收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息
  • 位置信息：我们不会收集您的地理位置信息
  • 设备信息：我们不会收集设备型号、操作系统版本等设备信息
  • 使用行为数据：我们不会收集您的使用行为、使用频率等数据

二、数据使用

本应用收集和存储的信息仅用于以下目的：

2.1 提供应用功能

  • 保存您的应用设置和偏好，以便下次使用时保持一致性
  • 实现图片上传、处理和导出功能
  • 支持颜色管理和自定义功能

2.2 改进用户体验

  • 根据您的设置优化应用性能
  • 提供个性化的使用体验

三、数据存储

3.1 本地存储

所有数据均存储在您的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器或云端。这意味着：

  • 您的数据完全由您控制
  • 我们无法访问您的数据
  • 数据不会在网络上传输，降低了数据泄露的风险

3.2 数据删除

您可以通过以下方式删除本地存储的数据：

  • 在应用设置中重置应用设置
  • 删除应用以清除所有本地存储的数据

四、第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方服务，包括：

  • 不使用第三方分析工具
  • 不使用第三方广告服务
  • 不使用第三方云存储服务
  • 不与任何第三方共享您的数据

五、数据安全

我们采取以下措施保护您的数据安全：

  • 本地处理：所有数据处理在本地完成，避免网络传输风险
  • 权限最小化：仅请求必要的系统权限
  • 数据隔离：应用数据与其他应用数据完全隔离

六、儿童隐私

本应用适合所有年龄段的用户使用。我们：

  • 不会故意收集未满18岁儿童的个人信息
  • 不会收集任何儿童的个人信息
  • 不提供任何可能对儿童有害的内容

七、数据跨境传输

由于本应用的所有数据都存储在您的设备本地，不涉及任何数据跨境传输。

八、隐私政策的更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在应用中公布。重大变更时，我们会通过应用内通知的方式告知您。

继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的隐私政策。

九、您的权利

根据适用的数据保护法律，您享有以下权利：

  • 访问权：您有权了解我们如何处理您的数据
  • 删除权：您有权要求删除您的数据
  • 更正权：您有权更正不准确的数据
  • 反对权：您有权反对某些数据处理活动

由于本应用的数据完全存储在本地，您可以通过删除应用或重置应用设置来行使上述权利。

BEAD STUDIO Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 29, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to BEAD STUDIO (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We understand the importance of privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to any part of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Locally Stored Data

The App is an offline application where all data processing is completed locally on your device and will not be uploaded to any server. We may store the following information on your device:

  • App Settings: Including grid size, color count, rendering mode, display mode, and other user preference settings
  • Color Override Settings: Your custom color override data
  • Temporary Processing Data: Temporary files and data generated during image processing

1.2 Permission Usage

To provide complete functionality, the App requires access to the following system permissions:

  • Photo Library Access: Used to upload images from the photo library and save exported works to the photo library
  • File System Access: Used to save and manage exported work files

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Identity Information: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or other personal identity information
  • Location Information: We do not collect your geographical location information
  • Device Information: We do not collect device model, operating system version, or other device information
  • Usage Behavior Data: We do not collect your usage behavior, usage frequency, or other data

2. Data Usage

The information collected and stored by the App is used only for the following purposes:

2.1 Providing App Functionality

  • Save your app settings and preferences for consistency on next use
  • Implement image upload, processing, and export functionality
  • Support color management and customization features

2.2 Improving User Experience

  • Optimize app performance based on your settings
  • Provide personalized user experience

3. Data Storage

3.1 Local Storage

All data is stored locally on your device and will not be uploaded to any server or cloud. This means:

  • Your data is completely under your control
  • We cannot access your data
  • Data is not transmitted over the network, reducing the risk of data leakage

3.2 Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored data by:

  • Resetting app settings in the app settings
  • Deleting the app to clear all locally stored data

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, including:

  • Does not use third-party analytics tools
  • Does not use third-party advertising services
  • Does not use third-party cloud storage services
  • Does not share your data with any third parties

5. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data security:

  • Local Processing: All data processing is completed locally, avoiding network transmission risks
  • Minimal Permissions: Only requests necessary system permissions
  • Data Isolation: App data is completely isolated from other app data

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We:

  • Do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 18
  • Do not collect any personal information from children
  • Do not provide any content that may be harmful to children

7. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Since all data of the App is stored locally on your device, no cross-border data transfer is involved.

8. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be announced in the App. For significant changes, we will notify you through in-app notifications.

Continued use of the App indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access Right: You have the right to know how we process your data
  • Deletion Right: You have the right to request deletion of your data
  • Correction Right: You have the right to correct inaccurate data
  • Objection Right: You have the right to object to certain data processing activities

Since the App's data is completely stored locally, you can exercise the above rights by deleting the App or resetting app settings.
